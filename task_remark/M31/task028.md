# Task 028 - M31 Release 1 purchase screens

Open Purchases from the paired workspace. Record a supplier invoice with product lines, invoice tax settings, discount and any payments already made. The purchase detail shows the recorded line values, tax split, applied payments and balance due.

Owners can preview corrections and removals against the current revision. The review shows changes to quantity, stock value, valuation variance, purchase debt and supplier advances. Confirmation uses the reviewed data and a stable posting key. A stale revision or changed preview requires a fresh review. Removed purchases and their before-and-after versions stay available to owners.

Managers can record purchases within their authorized branch and view active purchase details. Owner permissions are checked again on correction preview, confirmation and history. The existing purchase and correction services apply the same rules as the API.

See [test results and screenshots](test-results.md). Supplier-return controls are part of M31-R2 after M22. Next for Release 1 is M32, the POS cart and held bills.
