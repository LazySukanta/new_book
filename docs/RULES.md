## B. Money and calculations (R1â€“R10)

- R1: Monetary inputs have at most two fractional digits and use exact INR decimal strings, stored DECIMAL(18,2), serialized with two places such as "12.35". Reject extra input precision. Never binary floating point. Quantities retain up to three decimals and obey unit allow_decimal.
- R2: Exact decimal arithmetic with at least nine intermediate fractional places. Round monetary components half-up to INR 0.01 before persistence; average inventory unit cost retains nine places internally, but reported prices/cost amounts/taxes/payments use two. Validate range before storage.
- R3: Select retail/wholesale price server-side. Wholesale uses the highest qualifying slab minimum; retail ignores slabs. Explicit overrides require permission and audit. Product tax-inclusive flag applies to the selected price. Mixed inclusive/exclusive lines are calculated independently.
- R4: Extended price = quantity Ã— unit price. Round to two places. Line discount is an extended-line amount or percentage of extended price; it cannot exceed that price. Percentages use basis points (100 bp = 1%).
- R5: Allocate bill discount proportionally across discounted line amounts, using integer paise and largest remainder allocation, ties by stable line order. Allocated shares must sum exactly to the bill discount. Disallow discounts above the available amount.
- R6: Exclusive line: taxable = discounted amount. Inclusive line: taxable = discounted amount / (1 + rate_bp/10000). Tax equals inclusive amount minus taxable, or rounded taxable Ã— rate for exclusive lines. All values are nonnegative before reversal. Zero-rate lines have zero tax.
- R7: For an intra-state line, split total line tax into CGST and SGST at two places with deterministic residual assigned to SGST. Interstate lines use IGST only. The sum of tax components equals line tax; the sum of taxable plus tax equals line total. Header fields are sums of stored line fields, never independently recalculated tax on totals.
- R8: Round calculated line monetary components to two places, half-up; sum stored components for header amounts. Payable totals and payments have two places. Keep round_off at "0.00" unless a separately approved payable-rounding policy is configured; do not add hidden three-decimal residuals. Example: quantity 0.125 at price INR 12.35 gives 1.54375 intermediate, stored line amount INR 1.54 before tax. Statutory return/remittance rounding remains separate from invoice precision.
- R9: total = taxable + CGST + SGST + IGST + round_off. Discounts are already included in taxable and must not be subtracted again. Preview and posting use the same calculator, but posting revalidates current prices, tax and availability. Actual payments cannot exceed payable balance. Walk-in must settle in full.
- R10: Returns use original stored amounts and discounts, not current catalogue prices. For each component, cumulative returned amount = rounded(original component Ã— cumulative returned qty / sold qty); this return receives the difference from previous returns. Full cumulative returns restore original component amounts exactly. Distribute invoice round-off similarly across cumulative eligible returned line values with deterministic residual handling. Refunds settle in paise; record any interim settlement rounding explicitly and allocate residual on final return. Test partial returns in different orders before implementation approval.

## C. Stock and transaction integrity

1. Stock only changes through signed ledger entries, including admin changes. Balances are projections, never freely editable counters. Opening stock, damage, missing stock, corrections and found stock need reason, actor and value basis.
2. Admin may enter an absolute physical count or signed delta. Under a balance lock compute delta = count minus current quantity, save before/count/delta/after, and post the ledger entry in one transaction. Reject a stale count submission unless explicitly reconfirmed against the changed balance version.
3. Posted ledger entries and adjustments are immutable. Correct mistakes with new opposite entries linked to originals. Zero-difference physical counts with unchanged value may be audited without a zero-movement ledger row; an explicit cost correction with changed value requires a ledger entry.
4. Sales may make recorded stock negative when allow_negative_stock is enabled (default true). Warn rather than block; preserve estimated-cost provenance under section Q. Owner may disable this setting, restoring strict availability checks. Transfers, supplier returns and stock-removing corrections still require sufficient recorded stock. Positive manual stock corrections always require explicit unit cost. Stock is never backdated in v1.
5. Inventory value is authoritative alongside quantity. Receipts add acquisition value; ordinary issues remove average-cost value. Round value movement to two places; a depletion to exactly zero removes the remaining value; retain last known cost separately for future estimates. Crossing below zero follows section Q, not ordinary weighted-average division by nonpositive quantity. Transfers carry the source's actual issued value. Customer returns restore original issued value. Supplier returns remove current carrying value and separately report the difference from supplier credit value. Recoverable input GST is excluded from inventory cost; nonrecoverable tax is included, based on accountant-approved policy.
6. Every posting, cancellation, return, payment, reversal, adjustment and transfer state change is a DB transaction. Global lock order: idempotency row, affected branch rows sorted by ID (also serializes series allocation/configuration), affected party rows sorted by type/id, document rows sorted by type/id, catalogue pricing rows sorted by product/tax ID, stock balance rows sorted by branch/product, document_series rows sorted by ID. Discover required locks before mutation. Recheck quantities, statuses, credit and balances under lock. Concurrent first-use balance creation uses unique keys with safe retry. Bounded deadlock retries retain the same idempotency key.
7. Idempotency key mandatory for financial/stock posting. Same key and payload returns original result; different payload gives 409 IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT. Browser retries must not create duplicate documents. Failed transactions leave no partial effects.

## D. Finalization and cancellation

1. Held carts have no stock, payments, invoice number or debt. Completing a hold locks and finalizes the same sales.id exactly once, transitioning held to completed and recording completed_at/by. Retain held cart/pricing snapshots and save/edit history. Never delete/recreate it. Discard is held to discarded with actor/time/reason, also retained. Failure rolls back to held; all posting effects commit together.
2. Issued sale/return economic snapshots and all ledger movements are immutable. Owner purchase correction is the versioned exception defined in D.4/U: old purchase snapshots remain immutable while the current purchase projection can change. Cancelled invoice numbers remain reserved. No number is exposed before transaction commit.
3. Sale cancellation blocked if returns already exist. Otherwise reverse stock at original issued cost; undo invoice debt and detach associated receipt allocations atomically. Refund or retain actual paid funds as explicit customer credit. credit_adjust means retaining customer credit, not inventing a payment. Walk-in overpayment/credit retention is prohibited. Manager cancellation window uses branch-local calendar dates.
4. Owner may edit or soft-delete any old purchase through a versioned correction transaction (section U). Later movement alone does not block it. Keep original and every subsequent snapshot permanently, plus reasons/actors/timestamps and compensating stock/valuation/party/payment effects. Never require a fictitious supplier return to fix a typing error. Validate actual resulting stock, return and allocation dependencies; fail only on specific invalid outcomes.
5. Sales/purchase return quantities cannot exceed original quantity minus previous returns. Lock original items. Return settlement reduces open debt first, then creates party credit or an actual refund. For sales, cancellation and return are mutually exclusive correction paths. Owner purchase revisions/soft deletion with existing supplier returns follow section U dependency checks instead.

## E. Payments and credit

Amounts positive, direction explicit, one party and one branch per payment. Payment allocations cannot cross branches or parties. Customer credit limits are checked across all branches under a customer lock, including concurrent sales; authorized override records actor and reason. Cashier receives only in permitted branches. FIFO allocation uses effective date then ID among eligible opening dues and invoices in that branch; respect allocation effective dates and section P. Unallocated receipts are customer credit; unallocated supplier payments are advances. No cash-drawer/expense accounting module in this supplied scope.

A reversal is an append-only opposite-direction payment plus negative allocations; original remains visible and is marked reversed. Reports count the original and reversing row once each using their signs, rather than excluding the original and also subtracting its reversal. Prevent reversal of a reversal or double reversal. If funds/credit were subsequently used, block until dependent allocations/refunds are unwound in the same authorized workflow. Supplier refunds and customer refunds are distinct from new purchases/sales.

## F. GST and numbering

Resolve branch registration, actual place of supply and document type before calculating GST. Customer GSTIN state alone is insufficient to establish place of supply. Ordinary local counter sale defaults to branch state; delivery/interstate scenarios require explicit validated place of supply and audit for overrides. Registration and product tax treatment are separate concepts. Composition does not collect GST separately; unregistered bills are ordinary commercial bills. Mixed exempt/taxable, cess, reverse charge, export/SEZ and different-GSTIN transfers are deferred pending a specific design.

Store GST/HSN/party/address/registration/pricing snapshots. Historical tax amounts never change with settings or master edits. GSTIN structural validation is not government verification. Financial year starts 1 April in Asia/Kolkata. Invoice format MN1/26-27/A00001 is exactly 16 characters (3+1+5+1+6). Branch code at most three characters; invoice prefix one letter, counter five digits (1–99999). Exclude I/O from selectable prefixes. Letter ownership is configurable per branch/FY/type/channel; example allocations are not defaults or hard-coded reservations. All document numbers are at most 16 characters and globally unique in this installation, including distinct branches/types/channels.

Lock branch/series allocation before issuing. Infer owning channel from trusted server authentication, not arbitrary client input. Exhausted series moves only to the next ready allocation for that same branch, year, type and channel. Mark exhaustion and allocation atomically with posting; concurrent terminals cannot issue the same serial. Never steal another channel's series. If no ready allocation exists, stop with DOCUMENT_SERIES_EXHAUSTED; missing initial setup gives DOCUMENT_SERIES_UNAVAILABLE. Owner may allocate additional unused prefixes, never reset counters, wrap, reuse numbers or reassign previously used ownership. Exhaustion of all allowed prefixes blocks issuance.

Credit notes use MN1/26-27/X00001 (16 characters): one letter and five digits, same shape as invoices. Initially reserve X, Y and Z as configurable credit-note allocations. Each supports 99,999 notes. Rollover X99999 to Y00001, then Y99999 to Z00001, only within the owning channel. After Z99999, owner must allocate another unused letter to this branch/FY/type/channel; if none remain, block issuance. Never use another document type or channel reservation. Prefix uniqueness across branch/FY keeps rendered numbers disjoint. Preserve cancelled credit notes and reverse their effects transactionally under the existing dependency rules. Credit notes mean return/value-reduction documents; an udhaar sale remains a normal sales invoice.

Online issuance is consecutive within each series: allocate number, document, stock and payment inside one DB transaction. Rollback before issue releases the unexposed allocation; it does not create a cancelled bill. Every committed/exposed number stays in issued_documents permanently; abandonment after issuance is recorded as cancellation with reason. Preview/held carts never consume numbers. Do not promise impossible gap-free recovery of lost offline storage.

Provision new year rows explicitly; begin each new series at 00001, including credit notes. Never restart an existing year's series. Future offline planning requires exclusive device allocations within its channel, durable local issue/cancellation records, synchronization deduplication and a written loss-of-device incident procedure. Do not fabricate missing invoices or mark unknown lost invoices cancelled. Offline/device leasing implementation remains out of scope.

Documents-issued report: by registration, branch, financial year, type, series, channel and issue-date reporting period; first/last issued serial, total committed documents, cancelled count, net issued and unexplained gaps. Include cancelled documents in total, subtract them once for net, and exclude reserved unused capacity. Reporting cut-off determines cancellation status. This is a reporting aid, not automatic return filing.

Statutory rounding is not inferred from two-decimal storage. Review section 170 and applicable invoice/return rules with the accountant, recording the approved policy before live billing. Source: https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/CGST-Act-Updated-31082021.pdf .

## I. Transfers

Same GSTIN only until tax handling for distinct registrations is designed. Draft changes have no stock effect. Source-authorized actor dispatches; destination-authorized actor receives. Lock both branches' balances in global order. Dispatch removes source stock and creates in-transit quantity/value. Receipt adds only confirmed quantity to destination at carried value. One final receipt per transfer; missing goods remain in transit pending resolution, rather than disappearing.

For every item: dispatched = received + recovered + written_off + unresolved. Recovery adds destination stock; write-off reduces in-transit asset value and records loss without changing physical branch stock. Never create a second source deduction for a shortfall. Reject over-receipt/over-resolution and repeated dispatch or receipt. No dispatched transfer deletion/cancellation in this version. Valuation reports show branch stock and in-transit value separately.

## P. Gradual setup and historical financial records

1. Setup never blocks trading. Add products, customers, suppliers, dated opening dues, receipts, payments and advances gradually, including effective dates before first app use. No mandatory completed-opening checklist. Show not_entered separately from confirmed_zero per party/branch; either status still permits entries and billing. Incomplete debt data warns that credit/outstanding figures are incomplete, without bypassing existing credit-limit checks.
2. Financial effective dates may precede created_at and app launch. created_at/actor remain actual immutable entry history. Owner records/corrects historical financial entries and opening status in v1; corrections use signed reversal/replacement, reason and before/after audit. Later staff permission expansion can be configured explicitly. Future-dated payments/openings rejected.
3. Dated opening dues increase a party balance, credits/advances reduce it; no fake historical invoice, GST calculation or stock effect. Actual historical receipts/payments use their paid_at and normal direction. Unallocated advances can be applied later. Explicit application effective time cannot precede payment or debt effective time. Recompute open balances and historical running ledgers using effective dates, retaining actual entry chronology.
4. Opening balances are a net statement as of their date, not a second copy of underlying transactions. An earlier receipt already included must be explicitly tagged included_in_opening and linked to the matching opening entry, with zero additional balance effect and no allocation. Otherwise it is a normal-effect receipt, requiring confirmation of its new balance effect; adjust an incorrect opening with reversal/replacement. Never automatically subtract every pre-opening payment again. Show balance impact preview and duplicate-reference warnings.
5. An opening debt is allocatable like an invoice debt, under party/branch locks. Opening credit can settle an invoice using dated credit application records without changing party balance twice. Reversal respects consumed credit/payment dependencies. A zero opening is confirmed via status with actor/effective date, not a fake transaction. Historical payments are included in their effective-period cash/payment reports, not today's collection simply because entered today.
6. Late entries may change prior period reports. Date-range reports use effective time and offer recorded_as_of cut-off to reproduce what was recorded at a prior time. No retroactive stock or GST invoice issuance is implied by a historical receipt or balance. Old bill references may be retained as evidence only; a historical purchase invoice date can be stored while goods receipt/stock posting remains current.

## Q. Current-date stock corrections, estimates and prices

1. All stock postings use actual server posting time in v1, including opening stock and purchase receipts. No user-supplied earlier stock date. A historical supplier invoice date is a financial/reference date, not a backdated stock movement. Opening stock entered after sales is a current-date correction. Never replay/rewrite historical sale cost on later stock entry.
2. For an inventory shortage sold while enabled, flag the shortage cost_estimated. Estimate from last known branch cost, otherwise product purchase cost, otherwise explicit approved estimate, otherwise zero with source unknown_zero. Record the exact cost source and estimated quantity. If a line spans available and shortage units, sum the actual available carrying value and shortage estimate; flag the line and preserve its total and blended unit cost. All estimates remain visible even after stock becomes positive. No division by zero/negative quantity to compute average.
3. Negative stock value is provisional signed inventory at the captured estimate; ordinary negative-stock issues continue at the fallback estimate. Receipts arriving against negative stock clear the covered negative quantity/value prospectively; any difference between acquisition cost and prior provisional value is a current valuation variance. For a receipt partially clearing shortage and leaving positive stock, remaining positive units use receipt cost; if shortage remains, retain provisional value for outstanding shortage. Track variance explicitly rather than changing original sale COGS. Cost reports disclose both original estimated COGS and current variance separately.
4. Physical count correction: lock current balance; qty_change = counted_qty - qty_before. A stock addition requires explicit unit cost. For a count with explicit cost, target inventory value = count * unit_cost; value_change = target - value_before. Store valuation_variance = value_change - qty_change * unit_cost (rounded consistently). This adjustment reconciles valuation without changing original sales. Count at zero targets zero value; after a positive verified count use supplied cost for future issues. Audit before/after quantity/value, reason, actor, cost, variance and current posted_at. Corrections are immutable, reversed/corrected by new current entries.
5. Acceptance: 2 December sell three rice packs before stock is entered, qty -3, cost_estimated true. On 5 December count 47 with unit cost INR 40: qty change +50, closing value INR 1,880. If old estimate was INR 40, previous value -120, value change +2,000 and variance zero. If old cost was unknown/zero, previous value zero, value change +1,880 and variance -120. Both leave original invoices/cost flags unchanged. Show provisional gross profit and estimated-cost counts in reports; do not silently call zero estimated cost a verified profit.
6. Product selling/wholesale price, slab, name, cost and tax changes affect documents posted after the change only. Every sale/purchase/return line holds its own snapshot. Returns use original prices/tax/cost, including estimated-cost provenance. Financial reports and debt read persisted document components. Selling prices do not change stock quantities or inventory cost.
7. Held bills use current server-resolved prices/slabs/tax at completion. Compare held and last acknowledged values; show a notice and require acknowledgement before payment if changed. Stale acknowledgement fails PRICE_CHANGED without consuming a number or taking payment. Audit all price/slab/tax mutations with actor/time/old/new; product page shows this history. Future scheduled prices are outside v1.
8. Future checks: pre-launch dated opening due; receipt before/after opening with explicit inclusion; zero versus not-entered status; late entry and recorded_as_of reporting; partial settlement of opening debt; advance application dates; signed reversal conservation; negative-stock sale with known/unknown estimates; mixed available/shortage line; negative-to-positive receipt valuation; -3 to 47 count example; stale counts; unchanged old COGS; price history; held-price acknowledgement; original-price return.

## U. Owner-only purchase edits/deletion with permanent history

Owner is the only role allowed to change a posted purchase or delete it from the active view. Soft deletion retains rows, revisions, original line identities, audit and linked history indefinitely; no application endpoint permanently erases them. Every accepted version has an immutable purchase_revisions row with complete before/after snapshots, action, reason, actor, real timestamp, device and effect summary. Initial posting creates revision 1. Use expected_revision plus idempotency, row locks and one DB transaction for revised projection, stock/valuation deltas, supplier due adjustments, payment allocation entries and audit. No effect is committed on partial failure.

Do not use a blanket later-product-movement check. Metadata-only edits have no stock effect. Quantity changes post current-date differential ledger entries; cost changes post current valuation correction/variance with saved basis and owner preview. Do not rewrite old ledger rows, old sale invoices or historical sale costs. Current correction value and any estimated/consumed-cost variance are explicitly separated in reports. Preview must specify deterministic current stock/value effect before owner confirmation; require confirmed valuation inputs where original cost cannot determine a valid current adjustment, rather than silently revaluing unrelated stock. This is a correction workflow, not an actual supplier goods return.

Supplier debt uses revised purchase total once, while actual payment history stays intact. Excess paid funds become supplier advance or an explicitly recorded actual refund; never invent returned money. Signed allocation corrections are append-only. Supplier changes require detaching/reconciling old-party allocations and cannot change payment ownership. Existing returns stay linked to original line identity; cannot reduce bought quantity below already-returned quantity or orphan their product/supplier links. Preview/confirmation explains any required dependency resolution.

Block stock-removing corrections that would leave negative stock, or incompatible return/consumed-payment/credit outcomes, with a specific conflict and resolution guidance. Negative-stock sales do not bypass this correction check. Purchase deletion reverses only the purchase's remaining active contribution, accounting for previous revisions/returns; never reverse the initial full receipt twice. Current branch stock correction rules and no-stock-backdating still apply. Financial effective date and actual correction timestamp are stored separately; reported history can reproduce earlier versions via recorded_as_of. Restore, if offered, is a new owner revision with fresh checks, never removal of the delete event.

Historical rows are immutable evidence; only current purchase projections change. This exception does not authorize owners to rewrite finalized sales or their invoice numbers. Verify permission, all version retention, concurrent stale edits, idempotent retry, payment/supplier conservation, valid correction after later sale, no historical COGS changes and atomic rollback on invalid dependencies.

## V. Approved installation and enrollment procedure (M03–M04)

The local shop:setup command creates the first owner with a prompted password and issues a one-time browser pairing code. No default owner password or public registration. Setup refuses to create another owner. Additional devices require an authenticated owner to approve their branch grants. Local shop:pair-device recovery also requires owner credentials and approves all active branches; use the owner API for narrower grants.

Pairing codes expire in 30 minutes, are stored only as hashes, and are consumed once under a database lock. Initial login requires valid user credentials and a fresh code. Browser pairing establishes an encrypted HttpOnly cookie and rotated session; later browser login requires credentials and that cookie. Device UUID alone is never enrollment proof. Revoked devices cannot log in or use existing sessions/tokens. Current user, tenant and device activity and branch intersection are checked on every protected request.

Token enrollment consumes a fresh code and issues a 30-day Sanctum token bound to that user, tenant and device. It cannot switch tenant via headers. Current permissions remain authoritative. Fresh token enrollment currently requires fresh owner approval; automatic renewal and native channel enrollment await later client work. Current registration derives web platform/channel server-side. Browser UI uses session authentication and CSRF protection, not stored bearer tokens. Authentication and device audit records exclude passwords, codes, cookies and token contents.

## W. Purchase costing examples for M13

Use the supplier document's tax treatment and verified input-tax recoverability as separate inputs from the shop's selling settings. The actual bookstore registration, branch GSTINs and input-tax eligibility will be configured later. The following rates and outcomes are arithmetic fixtures for the purchase calculator.

- Ten units at an exclusive unit price of INR 10.00 with 18% intra-state tax produce taxable value INR 100.00, CGST INR 9.00, SGST INR 9.00 and payable INR 118.00. With fully recoverable input tax, inventory receives INR 100.00 at INR 10.000000000 per unit. With nonrecoverable input tax, it receives INR 118.00 at INR 11.800000000 per unit.
- Ten units priced at INR 11.80 inclusive give the same INR 118.00 payable and INR 100.00 taxable value. In the interstate fixture the full INR 18.00 is IGST. Payable remains INR 118.00 in either recoverability case.
- A taxable line of INR 1.01 at 5% rounds total tax to INR 0.05. CGST rounds half-up to INR 0.03 and SGST gets the INR 0.02 residual. A zero-rate line has zero tax. For supplier returns use original stored tax components and report the carrying-value difference separately.

## X. M11 purchase correction policy

The owner confirmed the remaining-unit rule. An owner correcting a previous purchase explicitly confirms the quantity still on hand before changing current inventory value. If no units remain, the owner confirms zero. Updating a cost for future purchases alone leaves earlier purchases, stock history and completed sales as recorded.

- Cost-only after consumption. Ten books were posted at INR 100 each and four have sold. If the owner confirms six units from that purchase remain on hand, correcting the unit cost to INR 110 increases inventory value by INR 60 and records INR 40 of extra acquisition cost as a current variance. On the stock ledger the signed variance is INR -40 under the inventory-change minus acquisition-change convention. The four sales retain their original costs. Confirmed quantity is capped by both retained purchase units and available stock. When none can be verified, the owner must explicitly confirm zero before allocating the full difference to variance.
- Quantity typo. A decrease from ten to eight units removes two units at current carrying cost and shows any difference from the supplier-cost change as variance. At INR 100 carrying and acquisition cost, the stock-value and supplier-total changes are both INR -200. If only one unit is on hand, block the removal and explain the required reconciliation.
- Supplier and payment. A purchase revised from INR 1,000 to INR 600 with INR 800 already paid leaves INR 200 as advance unless an actual refund is recorded. Moving the purchase to a different supplier preserves the original payment owner, detaches old allocations and previews the new supplier's full payable. An incompatible linked return blocks the move.
- Soft deletion. Preview the net quantity and value removal from the current revision after prior returns. Keep the original purchase, original line IDs and each revision. Block a removal that would leave negative stock or require an unresolved return or allocation correction.
